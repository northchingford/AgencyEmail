# AgencyEmail
Lists of agency domain names for your mail filtering needs.

Agencies are a nuisance.  We all hate them, but working in technology they're a necessary evil we have to go through to find an income.

In this repo you'll find lists in various formats of agency domain names which you can use to setup filtering rules, in order to keep their noise/spam away from your inbox.

These lists are not in any way meant to give a rating, hint at the trustworthiness nor cast aspersion at any business. 

You use these lists at your own risk.

If you want to reject certain domains, it has uses outside of filtering agency mail/spam/drivel, then have a look at the README_sender_access.md text document.  It provides an easy way to list domains to 554 block.
